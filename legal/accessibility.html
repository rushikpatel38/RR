Our commitment

R&R wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology. We have built this site with semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable menus and forms, visible focus states, and support for reduced-motion preferences.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We review and improve this website's accessibility over time as content and features change.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please let us know at [Business Email] so we can address it. Include the page and, if possible, the assistive technology you were using.
