Frequently asked questions

FAQ

What size of business do you work with?

We work with small businesses, startups, and mid-sized and enterprise organizations. Services are scoped to fit your size and complexity.

Do you offer one-time projects or only ongoing contracts?

Both. Some engagements are a defined project, like a migration or an assessment; others are ongoing, like managed IT or security monitoring.

How do we get started?

Contact us to schedule a consultation. Most engagements begin with an assessment of your current environment.

Can you work alongside our existing IT staff?

Yes. We regularly support internal IT teams rather than replace them, depending on what a business needs.

Is your team fully remote?

Yes, we're a fully remote team. Work is delivered remotely — on-site visits aren't part of how we operate.

What industries do you work with?

See our Industries page for the sectors we focus on. If yours isn't listed, reach out — many of our services apply broadly.

How do you price engagements?

It depends on scope. Defined projects are typically quoted up front; ongoing services like managed IT are priced as a recurring engagement. We'll walk through this during your consultation.

Do you sign NDAs or handle sensitive data?

Yes. We're comfortable signing NDAs and working with sensitive systems and data as part of normal engagements.

Have a question that's not answered here?

Reach out and we'll get back to you directly.
