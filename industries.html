Industries

Technology needs look different depending on what you run

A retail counter, a clinic, and a manufacturing floor don't share the same risks or requirements. Select an industry to see how we typically approach it.

Healthcare
Common challenges

Protecting patient records while keeping systems accessible to care teams across locations and devices.

Recommended solutions

Cloud infrastructure, managed IT support, and identity controls suited to clinical workflows.

Security considerations

Access controls, encryption, and monitoring appropriate for sensitive health information.

Cloud opportunities

Secure, available systems without maintaining on-site servers.

Financial Services
Common challenges

Protecting financial data and transactions while meeting expectations around risk and accountability.

Recommended solutions

Security assessments, risk management, and infrastructure built for resilience.

Security considerations

Strong identity and access management, monitoring, and incident response readiness.

Cloud opportunities

Scalable infrastructure with controls to match data sensitivity.

Retail
Common challenges

Keeping point-of-sale, inventory, and network systems running across one or many locations.

Recommended solutions

Network infrastructure, POS support, cloud tools, and business technology that scales per location.

Security considerations

Payment system security and network segmentation between POS and general business traffic.

Cloud opportunities

Centralized visibility across multiple store locations.

Restaurants & Hospitality
Common challenges

Network and POS infrastructure that needs to stay up during service, with limited on-site IT staff.

Recommended solutions

Managed network, POS infrastructure support, and remote IT support for fast issue resolution.

Security considerations

Securing guest Wi-Fi separately from POS and back-office systems.

Cloud opportunities

Cloud-based systems that keep operating even if a single location has issues.

Gas Stations & Convenience Businesses
Common challenges

Keeping payment systems, connectivity, and monitoring reliable across dispersed sites.

Recommended solutions

Network security, POS infrastructure, monitoring, and dependable connectivity.

Security considerations

Protecting payment infrastructure and monitoring for tampering or network intrusion.

Cloud opportunities

Centralized monitoring across multiple locations from one place.

Manufacturing
Common challenges

Connecting operational technology and business systems without creating new security gaps.

Recommended solutions

Infrastructure, network security, automation, and cloud tools for production and back-office systems.

Security considerations

Segmenting operational technology networks from general business IT.

Cloud opportunities

Better visibility into production and supply data without new on-site hardware.

Professional Services
Common challenges

Supporting client-facing teams that depend on email, files, and business applications working reliably.

Recommended solutions

Managed IT, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365 support, cloud, and business applications.

Security considerations

Protecting client data and communications, including access controls for shared files.

Cloud opportunities

Secure remote access for hybrid or distributed teams.

Small & Mid-Sized Businesses
Common challenges

Needing dependable, secure technology without the budget for a large internal IT department.

Recommended solutions

Right-sized managed IT, cloud, and security services that scale as the business grows.

Security considerations

Foundational protections — identity, endpoint, and backup — that are often skipped early on.

Cloud opportunities

Enterprise-grade tools without enterprise-scale infrastructure costs.
